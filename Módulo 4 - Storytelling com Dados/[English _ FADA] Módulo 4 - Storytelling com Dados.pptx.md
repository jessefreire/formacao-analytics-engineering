###### What is data storytelling? **CREATIVE PROCESS vs. ANALYTICAL PROCESS** 

> **Means** : Plotting a graph in spreadsheet or business intelligence software takes only a few seconds. However, understanding which graph, in which context, and for which audience is what requires our greatest effort. 

> **Process** : The way we communicate and who we communicate to requires creativity. We move away from the analytical process, which proves something based on data, and move on to the creative process, where we generate action and give meaning to the data.
